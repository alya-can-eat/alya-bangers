# FAQ

[‹ alya-bangers](../README.md)

**Why does the music get converted first?**

So the DS doesn't have to do it while playing. MP3, FLAC or Opus would keep the DS busy decoding all the time. The converted songs are ready to play as they are, so there's more power left for the screens, features and effects, the battery lasts longer, and it sounds better. More in [How playback works](how-it-works.md).

**Does everything get converted again every time?**

No, only new and changed songs. See [Converter](converter.md).

**How much fits on the card?**

The converted songs are big, about 30 MB for a 4 minute song:

| Card | Songs (about 4 min each) | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| 32 GB | about 1'000 | about 65 |
| 64 GB | about 2'000 | about 135 |
| 128 GB | about 4'000 | about 270 |

**Which formats work?**

FLAC, Opus, MP3, AAC/M4A, Vorbis, WAV, AIFF and WavPack, with their tags and covers. The converter does it all, no other tools needed.

**Can MP3s just be copied onto the card?**

No, alya-bangers only plays converted songs. MoonShell plays MP3 straight from the card, if that's what you need.
